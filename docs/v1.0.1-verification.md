## Scientific regression evidence

The PyPI case is a negative boundary regression. Public records for LastPyMile
(DOI `10.1145/3468264.3468592`) and the documented No Snake Oil build-verification
work show why a same-version artifact hash or file-set difference cannot be
promoted to a new mechanism without a discriminating claim and full-text
nearest-work review. The repository records this as a review boundary and
requires evidence; it does not hard-code a `NO_GO` answer.

The positive path is the bounded boundary route: a host must propose a falsible
condition, two rivals, an ideal result and a minimal discriminating pilot after
the retained literature regions are reviewed. The local `text_delta` pilot
executes that kind of evidence-preserving comparison. Its descriptive output
is exploratory and cannot be presented as an independent scientific discovery.

## Handoff status

The generated dossier reports `FORMAT_COMPATIBLE` and, when the locked
read-only adapter succeeds, `TOOLS_VERIFIED`. It always reports
`SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; V4.1 has final authority over novelty, feasibility,
protocol, statistics, reproducibility and publication sufficiency. A missing,
changed or non-portable artifact yields `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`.

## Known limits

- No automatic PDF acquisition or access-control bypass is implemented.
- Host model reasoning is only verified when a real response supplies actor,
  model, request hash and artifacts.
- The process governor is not a security sandbox and does not isolate network,
  filesystem or native-code access.
- External provider quotas and V4.1 host availability remain runtime
  conditions; failed attempts are retained as errors.
